COMMON INQUIRIES

Frequently Asked Electrical Questions

Everything you need to know about safety codes, project timelines, warranties, and emergency dispatch.

Homes older than 35-40 years frequently retain cloth-insulated, knob-and-tube, or ungrounded wiring. Signs include persistent breaker trips, warm outlet cover plates, burning smells, flickering lighting, or lack of three-prong grounded receptacles. Our non-invasive thermal audit can definitively verify your system's integrity.
Standard breakers only protect against over-current and dead shorts. GFCI (Ground Fault Circuit Interrupter) defends against electrical shock in wet areas like kitchens and bathrooms. AFCI (Arc Fault Circuit Interrupter) uses microprocessors to detect hazardous electrical sparking behind walls—the primary cause of residential electrical fires.

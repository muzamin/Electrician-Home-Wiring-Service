Engineering Standard

Master Electrical Engineering & Fire Prevention

Electrical infrastructure is the circulatory system of modern living. We build residential systems with the exact safety margins, thermal tolerances, and conductor ratings required for heavy induction appliances and EV charging.

100% Oxygen-Free Solid Copper Conductors

We never use cheap copper-clad aluminum (CCA). All wiring uses certified 99.9% pure copper for zero heat buildup.

Fluke® Infrared Thermal Scanning

Every terminal and junction is scanned under peak load with calibrated thermal cameras to identify micro-arcing before it starts.

Arc-Fault Circuit Interrupter (AFCI) Standard

Advanced electronic sensors detect hazardous electrical sparks within 2 milliseconds to immediately disconnect power.
